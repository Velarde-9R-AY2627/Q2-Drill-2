About OB. MONTESSORI CENTER

Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center (OBMC) is dedicated to nurturing young minds through the holistic educational methodology developed by Dr. Maria Montessori.

As a premier pre-K to Grade 12 educational institution in the Philippines, OBMC blends proven traditions with modern innovative learning strategies to prepare students for real-world leadership and service.

Our mission is to transform our students into self-developing, self-disciplined, and productive individuals ready to make a positive impact in an ever-changing world.
